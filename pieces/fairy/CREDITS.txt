The Ouroboros King pieces (crusader, viking, berserker, bowman, catapult, sorcerer, manticore, gryphon,
minotaur, hydra, phoenix, whelp, wyrm, cardinal, warwagon, bladedancer, musketeer, templar, royalguard,
prince, immortal, assassin, firechick, egg, agentplus, agentx, agentl, agentstar, decoy, fool, infiltrator,
marchingpawn, princess, leper, glassqueen, mirrorqueen, portalmage, troll, trollleft, reaper, bannerman,
gorgon, martyr, quartermaster, mountedking, general, tabitha, andromeda, pegasus) are own drawings in the
style of the set, made by tools/draw_ouroboros_pieces.py.

Redrawn on 2026-10-01 after the original units' designs (all 49 pieces): each picture now follows the
silhouette, pose and key features of the unit as The Ouroboros King shows it (crown in a laurel wreath for
the prince, virus for the leper, jester hat for the fool, great helm for the knight templar, hooded figure
with crossed blades for the blade dancer and so on). Every shape is drawn from scratch as vector paths in
the cburnett style; no art from the game was used, traced or embedded. The pegasus rider reuses
the cburnett knight outline (CC BY-SA 3.0). Agent +, Agent X, Agent L and Agent * kept their first drawings
(the cburnett rook, bishop, knight and queen outlines with a sign, CC BY-SA 3.0), which the user preferred.
checker, checkerking: drawn for Power Chess (2026-10-02), plain flat draughts discs with one inner ring, a crown for the king.
devil, demon, slime, blob, duck_yellow, duck_blue: drawn for Power Chess (2026-10-03) in the same 45x45 line style; the duck after the usual Duck Chess duck (side view, yellow, orange bill).
shotgunking, bosspawn, bossqueen, bossking: drawn for Power Chess (2026-10-03), Shotgun King mode (after Shotgun King: The Final Checkmate, own drawings).
gun_solomon, gun_victoria, gun_richard, gun_makeda, gun_alexander, gun_yvan, gun_ramesses, gun_montezuma, gun_attila: drawn for Power Chess (2026-10-03), one gun per Shotgun King shotgun, laid over the normal king of the piece set in use. w_/b_shotgunking.svg = the default set's king plus gun_solomon.
