Other kinds of chess: new rules, new pieces, other board sizes, or a variant you define yourself. Stockfish 19 cannot play these, so your opponent is Fairy-Stockfish.

Each piece is one line, for example customPiece1 = w:NN. The part after the colon is Betza notation: N knight, B bishop, R rook, Q queen, K king, W one step straight, F one step diagonal, C camel, Z zebra. A doubled letter keeps going in the same direction. A comment line # glyphs: w=N+N draws w as a knight with a knight badge.
